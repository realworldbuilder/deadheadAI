Common questions

The music won’t play. Audio streams live from archive.org, so you need a network connection. If the Archive itself is having a rough night (it happens), the app shows a banner and playback comes back when they do.

Do I need an account? No. Tap “Hop on the Bus” and everything works — search, guides, journeys, recommendations — powered by the built-in offline knowledge base. Signing in with Apple unlocks the full AI brain (free) and backs your shelves & journal up to your own iCloud.

My shelves didn’t follow me to a new phone. Shelves and journal entries sync through iCloud only when you’re signed in with Apple, on both devices, with iCloud enabled. Everything else (listening history, taste profile) intentionally stays on the device it happened on.

Where does the music come from? Every recording streams straight from the Internet Archive’s Grateful Dead collection — the band has allowed taping and free trading of their live shows since the seventies, and the Archive keeps the flame. The app stores and hosts nothing; support them if you can.

Delete my data? Delete the app and everything on-device goes with it. iCloud copies of your shelves & journal can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Deadhead AI. Details in the privacy policy.
