Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

The short version: your listening lives on your phone, your shelves can live in your iCloud, your questions go to OpenAI only to get answered, and nobody here is tracking, profiling, or selling anything. There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Listening history and the taste profile computed from it
  • Journey progress and AI chat transcripts
  • Your display name and, if you sign in with Apple, the Apple-provided user identifier
  • Cached setlists and search results (so the app works offline)

None of this is transmitted to us. We have no servers and no ability to see it.

What syncs to your iCloud (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, your collections (saved shows) and journal entries sync to the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. Apple encrypts and stores this data under your Apple ID; we cannot read, access, or share it. Sign out and syncing stops; your data stays on the device and in your iCloud until you remove it (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Deadhead AI).

What gets sent to OpenAI

When the AI brain is active, the text of your chats and searches — plus non-identifying context like era preferences and candidate show listings — is sent to OpenAI’s API to generate responses. These requests carry no account identifier, name, or device identifier. Per OpenAI’s API policy, API data is not used to train their models. Without sign-in, an offline knowledge base answers instead and nothing is sent at all.

What archive.org sees

Recordings stream directly from the Internet Archive, so archive.org receives the standard information any web request carries (your IP address and the items you stream), governed by the Internet Archive’s terms and privacy policy. We are not a party to that traffic.

Sign in with Apple

Used solely to unlock the hosted AI mode and iCloud sync. We receive the name you choose to share and an anonymous Apple user identifier; both stay on your device. No account is created on any server of ours, because we don’t have any.

Children

The app is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because we do not collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
